| Aspect                         | Details                                                                                                                                                              |
| :----------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ancient Classification**     | Based on whether the soil was fertile (**Urvara**) or sterile (**Usara**).                                                                                             |
| **Modern Classification Bodies** | <ul><li>Soil Survey of India (established in 1956).</li><li>National Bureau of Soil Survey and Land Use Planning (NBSS&LUP) under the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR).</li></ul> |
| **Soil Map**              | ![Soil Map of India \| Types of Soils in India \| UPSC](https://www.mapsofindia.com/maps/india/india-map-of-soils.jpg)                                                  |

## **Major Types of Soil in India**

### **1. Alluvial Soil 🌱**
| Feature          | Details                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Image                                                                                                                                                              |
| :--------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Area**         | <ul><li>Most widespread soil in India (about 43% of total area, covering ~143 sq km).</li><li>Covers the largest area among Indian soils.</li></ul>                                                                                 | ![Alluvial Soil \| Types of Soil in India \| UPSC](https://www.upscprep.com/content/images/2024/05/1.png)                                                      |
| **Distribution** | <ul><li>Northern plains (Indo-Gangetic plains) and river valleys.</li><li>Peninsular India: deltas (e.g., Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri) and estuaries.</li><li>Rajasthan, Gujarat (narrow corridor).</li></ul>                     | ![Major Soil Types \| Alluvial Soil \| UPSC](https://lh7-us.googleusercontent.com/RmmrbFLPSTILgbxluBWYpbDGMMscl3t7VItxAyTMaVKgb5nDiGdlOoHdENFVbeaa4vnV3c0L_qWgC3UNfpsHk0TjVAqhCaci0u1BzjTSozv45IBhZxDdvR1-iG2Np0VHoW3v75cWUC7N3OuNf9ehiY8) |
| **Nature**       | <ul><li>Depositional soil: transported and deposited by rivers, streams.</li><li>**Khadar**: New alluvium, fine-textured, more fertile, found in floodplains, replenished annually.</li><li>**Bhangar**: Old alluvium, coarser, contains 'kankar' (calcium carbonate nodules), less fertile, found on higher terraces.</li></ul> |                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Composition**  | <ul><li>Rich in: humus (variable), potash, lime, organic matter.</li><li>Poor in: phosphorus, often nitrogen.</li></ul>                                                                                                                |                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Fertility**    | <ul><li>Highly fertile, especially Khadar.</li><li>Respond well to irrigation and fertilizers.</li></ul>                                                                                                                                |                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Color & Texture**| <ul><li>Color: Light grey to ash grey.</li><li>Texture: Sandy loam to clayey.</li></ul>                                                                                                                                             |                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Crops**        | <ul><li>Wheat, rice, maize, sugarcane, pulses, oilseed, jute, etc.</li><li>Supports intensive agriculture.</li></ul>                                                                                                                   |                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **UPSC/NCERT Notes** | <ul><li>Formed mainly due to silt deposited by Indo-Gangetic-Brahmaputra rivers.</li><li>Also occur in Narmada & Tapi valleys.</li><li>Can be divided into Inland Alluvial (plains, valleys) and Coastal Alluvial (deltas, coastal strips).</li></ul> |                                                                                                                                                                    |

### **2. Red Soil ❤️ (often includes Yellow Soil)**
| Feature              | Details                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Image                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| :------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Area**             | <ul><li>Second largest soil group in India (approx. 18.5% of total area).</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                        | ![Red Soil \| Types of Soil in India \| UPSC](https://www.upscprep.com/content/images/2024/05/2.png)                                                                                                                                  |
| **Formation**        | <ul><li>Develops on crystalline igneous rocks (like granite, gneiss) and metamorphic rocks.</li><li>Formed in ==low-rainfall areas of the Deccan Plateau.==</li></ul>                                                                                                                      | ![Major Soil Types \| Red Soil \| UPSC](https://lh7-us.googleusercontent.com/TI99HXbdG_WPIW-aZxaCUT2l0QDYz2hLtqyVRNVcnyHBongDWQo96vAsO0pZ6pTsIXPpVOqplNRSY3Ds0LRDxLSlRRIRDMAwyCZjKze0heP6hwxDoI2U1Q-qmwG153KMhf4hhJAybJBum5HMdgKOLFQ) |
| **Distribution**     | <ul><li>Eastern and Southern parts of Deccan Plateau.</li><li>Parts of Odisha, Chhattisgarh, southern parts of middle Ganga plain, Western Ghats ([[Piedmont zone\|Piedmont zone]] of western ghats).</li><li>Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, SE Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Jharkhand.</li></ul> |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Structure**        | <ul><li>Porous and friable (crumbly).</li><li>Not very water retentive.</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                          |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Composition**      | <ul><li>Rich in: iron, potash (variable).</li><li>Deficient in: lime, phosphate, manganese, nitrogen, humus, calcium carbonate.</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                  |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Color**            | <ul><li>Red color due to diffusion of ferric oxide.</li><li>Appears yellowish when it occurs in a hydrated form (often referred to as Yellow Soil).</li></ul>                                                                                                                              |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Fertility**        | <ul><li>Generally poor fertility but can be productive with fertilizers and irrigation.</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                          |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Crops**            | <ul><li>Wheat, cotton, pulses, tobacco, oilseeds, potatoes, millets, groundnut, ragi.</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                            |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **UPSC/NCERT Notes** | <ul><li>The fine-grained red and yellow soils are normally fertile, while coarse-grained soils in dry upland areas are poor in fertility.</li></ul>                                                                                                                                        |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

### **3. Black Soil / Regur Soil ⚫**
| Feature              | Details                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Image                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| :------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Area**             | <ul><li>Covers about 15% of India's total area.</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | ![Black Soil \| Types of Soil in India \| UPSC](https://www.upscprep.com/content/images/2024/05/3.png)                                                                                                                                               |
| **Other Names**      | <ul><li>Regur Soil (meaning cotton - best soil for cotton).</li><li>Cotton Soil.</li><li>Tropical Chernozems / Tropical Black Earths (internationally).</li></ul>                                                                                                             | ![Major Soil Types \| Black Soil / Regur soil \| UPSC](https://lh7-us.googleusercontent.com/m8uKtNcyoHhGgwF5oRPVu8vLxBoequkqfGvkwadYub6Qm3xKLDVIhhuY36uO9ov9Qr7Gv2aatmcobSubdG9fGg8hwnKpuLrbIjIdrlMiiXpkNkn0flpFoextdAPO9Te4oJxXRX3v4S50N9yGGh_0wUM) |
| **Formation**        | <ul><li>Derived from weathering of [[Basaltic Magma\|Basaltic Magma]] rocks of the Deccan Trap.</li><li>Structural soil (formed in situ).</li></ul>                                                                                                                                           |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Distribution**     | <ul><li>Deccan Plateau: Maharashtra (major part), Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, parts of Tamil Nadu, Karnataka.</li><li>Malwa Plateau.</li><li>Valleys of Krishna and Godavari.</li></ul>                                                               |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Characteristics**  | <ul><li>High water retaining capacity.</li><li>Swells and becomes sticky when wet; shrinks and develops deep cracks when dried (characteristic of self-plowing/self-aeration).</li><li>Clayey, deep, and impermeable.</li><li>Slow absorption and loss of moisture.</li></ul> |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Composition**      | <ul><li>Rich in: iron, lime, calcium carbonate, potassium, aluminum, magnesium carbonates.</li><li>Deficient in: nitrogen, phosphorus, organic matter.</li></ul>                                                                                                              |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Color Variation**  | <ul><li>Generally deep black. The source's note on "south to north along western coastal plains" for color variation seems unusual; typically, variations are within the Deccan region itself.</li></ul>                                                                      |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Crops**            | <ul><li>Cotton (main), sugarcane, jowar, tobacco, wheat, rice, oilseeds, citrus fruits, vegetables.</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                 |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **UPSC/NCERT Notes** | <ul><li>The parent material is volcanic rocks of Deccan Trap.</li><li>The self-ploughing characteristic helps in aeration.</li></ul>                                                                                                                                          |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

### **4. Laterite Soil 🧱**
| Feature              | Details                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Image                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| :------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Area**             | <ul><li>About 3.7% of India's area.</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | ![Laterite Soil \| Types of Soil in India \| UPSC](https://www.upscprep.com/content/images/2024/05/4.png)                                                                                                                                  |
| **Etymology**        | Derived from Latin word ‘Later’ (meaning brick).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | ![Major Soil Types \| Laterite Soil \| UPSC](https://lh7-us.googleusercontent.com/c9jHw2oj3rAk51YyJ0tXaEmb-GmJ865kTjvm-GfZSUnv3_zWmDH4Buk55u0cSxTzVdHGIVBNqmj3FlutaT99YuKm2XhFUL6YEmwHwPIFJw5pbJWAA2PMDyDr4wKqQDqQx4XPiDnYBcQ_wSi0FCGhLNM) |
| **Formation**        | <ul><li>Formed in areas of high temperature and high rainfall with alternate wet and dry periods.</li><li>Result of intense leaching (desilication) due to tropical rains, where lime and silica are leached away.</li><li>Bacteria thrive at high temperatures, consuming humus quickly.</li></ul> |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Distribution**     | <ul><li>Hilly areas of Western Ghats, Eastern Ghats, Rajmahal Hills, Satpura, Vindhya.</li><li>Patches in Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Madhya Pradesh, Odisha, Assam.</li></ul>                                                                                                                   |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Characteristics**  | <ul><li>Becomes soft when wet and very hard when dried (==can be cut into bricks==).</li><li>Acidic in nature (pH < 6.0).</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                 |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Composition**      | <ul><li>Rich in: Iron oxide and aluminum compounds (bauxite is often found).</li><li>Deficient in: humus content, nitrogen, phosphate, potash, lime.</li></ul>                                                                                                                                      |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Color**            | <ul><li>Red color due to iron oxide.</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Fertility**        | <ul><li>Generally infertile.</li><li>Respond to manuring and irrigation.</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Crops**            | <ul><li>Not suitable for most agriculture without amendments.</li><li>With manuring: Rice, Ragi, Sugarcane, Cashew nuts.</li><li>Suitable for plantation crops: tea, coffee, rubber, cinchona, coconut (especially in Kerala, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu).</li></ul>                                     |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **UPSC/NCERT Notes** | <ul><li>Found on high flat erosion surfaces.</li><li>Two types: Upland laterites (thin, gravelly, poor) and Lowland laterites (thick, clayey, richer).</li></ul>                                                                                                                                    |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

### **5. Desert / Arid Soil 🏜️**
| Feature          | Details                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Image                                                                                                                                                                             |
| :--------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Area**         | <ul><li>Around 4.42% of India's total area.</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                  | ![Desert and Arid Soil \| types of soil in India \| UPSC](https://www.upscprep.com/content/images/2024/05/5.png)                                                                     |
| **Nature**       | <ul><li>Gradational and depositional soils (aeolian - wind deposited).</li><li>Formed due to wind activities under arid and semi-arid conditions.</li></ul>                                                                               | ![Major Soil Types \| Desert / Arid Soil \| UPSC](https://lh7-us.googleusercontent.com/Uz8AhFFUM-XjgQU3polXT4S_52dUUG5ApMbCIm6cTvtEiehcps1yOiiWgIHRG0tX0kgwnbXS-uTCqnYnddzW3dKXIhI4uiH8ZeuLYOFrtkXeRwimPW5ypGlGELUo_Wq7kFZqp50CdBp6SsOcjfHj4V0) |
| **Distribution** | <ul><li>Western Rajasthan, parts of Haryana, Punjab, and Gujarat (Rann of Kutch).</li></ul>                                                                                                                                             |                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Characteristics** | <ul><li>Lacks moisture and humus (due to high temperature, rapid evaporation, and sparse vegetation).</li><li>High salt content (some obtained by evaporation).</li><li>'Kankar' (calcium carbonate) layer in lower horizons restricts water infiltration.</li><li>Alkaline in nature due to lack of leaching.</li></ul> |                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Composition**  | <ul><li>Nitrogen is insufficient.</li><li>Phosphate is normal/adequate.</li><li>Rich in soluble salts.</li></ul>                                                                                                                        |                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Color & Texture**| <ul><li>Color: Red to brown.</li><li>Texture: Sandy to gravelly, low density.</li></ul>                                                                                                                                               |                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Fertility**    | <ul><li>Poor in humus and organic matter.</li><li>Can be productive with irrigation (e.g., Indira Gandhi Canal).</li></ul>                                                                                                                 |                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Crops**        | <ul><li>Drought-resistant crops: bajra, pulses, fodder, guar, jowar, millets.</li><li>With irrigation: wheat, cotton, maize, mustard.</li></ul>                                                                                         |                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **UPSC/NCERT Notes** | <ul><li>The kankar layer formation in the bottom horizons restricts water penetration. When irrigation is available, soil moisture is readily available for sustainable plant growth.</li></ul> |                                                                                                                                                                                   |

## **Minor Types of Soil in India**

### **6. Forest Soil 🌲 (or Mountain Soil)**
| Feature          | Details                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Image                                                                                                                                                                     |
| :--------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Distribution** | <ul><li>Found in higher altitudes, hilly and mountainous areas with sufficient rainfall and dense forests (Himalayan region, Western and Eastern Ghats, some parts of Peninsular plateau).</li><li>Covers about 8% of India's area.</li></ul> | ![Forest Soil \| Types of Soil in India \| UPSC](https://www.upscprep.com/content/images/2024/05/6-2.png)                                                                             |
| **Characteristics** | <ul><li>Highly heterogeneous, depending on parent rock, altitude, vegetation, and climate.</li><li>Generally acidic with a shallow topsoil layer.</li><li>Humus content varies: low in coniferous belt (slow decomposition), rich in deciduous forest areas.</li><li>Texture: Loamy and silty in valley sides, coarse-grained in upper slopes.</li><li>Prone to soil erosion.</li></ul> | ![Major Soil types \| Forest Soil \| UPSC](https://lh7-us.googleusercontent.com/NNMhUVYH3ShtdFJLpDNSX0HnRag0Yito4iO0e9usX7L1rGW3U9cyRSnIodQnHshuCHzrs5XxyoU09SePhnfYPwQptG6mvgwfak0Vfg_XH1rNcObGKkdN_255njKcnUHWdzKfNjfIinbh0L07kT1Er4o) |
| **Fertility**    | <ul><li>Varies: fertile in lower valleys and river terraces/alluvial fans.</li><li>Often less fertile on steep slopes.</li><li>Low in potash, phosphorus, and lime.</li></ul>                                                            |                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Suitability & Crops** | <ul><li>Not suitable for intensive agriculture on slopes.</li><li>Suitable for: horticulture (fruits like apples, plums, peaches), tea, coffee, spices, temperate crops like potatoes, maize, barley.</li></ul>                        |                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **UPSC/NCERT Notes** | <ul><li>In snow-covered Himalayan areas, these soils experience denudation and are acidic with low humus.</li><li>Soils in lower valleys are often fertile.</li></ul>                                                                 |                                                                                                                                                                           |

| **Formation**        | <ul><li>**Saline soils:** Accumulation of soluble salts (chlorides, sulphates of sodium, calcium, magnesium) due to poor drainage, high evaporation in arid/semi-arid areas, seawater intrusion in coastal areas, over-irrigation causing capillary action bringing salts to surface.</li><li>**Alkaline soils:** Predominance of sodium carbonate/bicarbonate, leading to high alkalinity (high pH).</li></ul> | ![Major Soil types \| Saline and Alkaline Soils \| UPSC](https://lh7-us.googleusercontent.com/TQAKWSUX2eRwobCZjDu0GaemmwPcc2WCH5K_5zsgiE_2ho8sCxo-h3mRKmOL8S2H36DX_49U8xXnmUDVWMGeMUzbD9Qi5wQQmEY_-l0jSi_Y4jxRhFng8LPqqitPs1kfnsy44V3ikXGfVdR2wZ1a8XY)                |
| **Distribution**     | <ul><li>Arid and semi-arid regions: Rajasthan, Haryana, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Maharashtra, Gujarat (==Rann of Kutch== has extensive saline marshy soils).</li><li>Waterlogged and swampy areas.</li><li>Areas with intensive cultivation and excessive irrigation.</li></ul>                                                                                                                            |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Characteristics**  | <ul><li>High concentration of soluble salts on the surface (white encrustation).</li><li>Infertile and unsuitable for most crops.</li><li>Poor physical structure, low infiltration capacity.</li><li>Deficient in nitrogen and calcium.</li><li>Texture: Sandy to loamy sand.</li></ul>                                                                                                                        |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Reclamation**      | <ul><li>Improved drainage to leach out excess salts.</li><li>Application of gypsum (calcium sulphate) to reclaim alkaline/sodic soils (replaces sodium ions).</li><li>Cultivation of salt-tolerant crops (e.g., berseem, dhaincha, rice).</li><li>Flushing with excess water.</li><li>Adding organic matter.</li><li>Source mentions NDDB reclamation for fodder crops.</li></ul>                               |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Crops**            | <ul><li>Generally unsuitable.</li><li>Salt-tolerant: Barley, cotton, rice (salt-resistant varieties), sugarcane, berseem, fodder crops, certain vegetables and fruits like date palm, guava.</li></ul>                                                                                                                                                                                                          |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
