## Detailed Class Notes: Factors Affecting Indian Monsoon (Part 2) 📝

### **Recap: Monsoon Basics** 💨🌏

*   **Monsoon kya hai?** Ye **surface winds** hain.
*   **Origin kahan se?** Southern Hemisphere ke **Subtropical High Pressure Belt (STHPB)** se, specifically **Mascarene High** se.
*   **Flow Pattern:**
    *   **South East Trade Winds:** Mascarene High se ITCZ ki taraf flow karti hain.
    *   **Summer Mein Shift:** Jaise-jaise garmi badhti hai ☀️:
        *   **ITCZ (Inter Tropical Convergence Zone)** North ki taraf shift hota hai.
        *   SE Trade Winds bhi North aati hain.
        *   Equator cross karke, **Coriolis effect** ke kaaran right deflect hoti hain.
        *   India mein **South Westerly Winds** ban kar enter karti hain. ➡️ SW
    *   **Vertical Motion:** ITCZ pe ye hawa upar uthti hai (convection). Isse pura **circulation cell** complete hota hai. ⬆️

*   **Hadley Cell Connection:**
    *   Southern Hemisphere mein STHPB aur ITCZ ke beech ka cell = **Southern Hemisphere Hadley Cell**.
    *   Jab ITCZ North mein shift hota hai, toh essentially Southern Hemisphere ka Hadley Cell hi Northward extend ho jaata hai.
    *   Ye **Extended Hadley Cell** hi **Monsoon Cell** hai. 🔄
    *   **Challenge:** Ye cell ab almost double distance cover kar raha hai, isliye isko sustain karne ke liye extra factors chahiye.
    *   **Helping Factors:**
        *   **Somali Jet** (Part 1 mein cover kiya tha) - Surface winds ko push karta hai.
        *   **Subtropical Easterly Jet Stream (STEJ)** (Iss video ka topic) - Upper air circulation ko help karta hai. 👇 Aaj isko samjhenge.

### **Subtropical Easterly Jet Stream (STEJ) ka Formation** 🤔🌬️

*   **Winter Scene:** 🥶
    *   ITCZ Southern Hemisphere mein hota hai.
    *   **STHPB** aur **Subtropical Jet Stream (STJ - Westerly)** Northern Hemisphere mein hote hain, lagbhag Tropic of Cancer ke aas paas.

*   **Summer Transition:** ☀️
    *   Sun Northern Hemisphere mein aata hai.
    *   Lekin STHPB aur STJ *turant* shift nahi hote.
    *   Jahan STHPB hai, wahan सूरज ki kiranen lagbhag vertical padti hain.

*   **Heating Under High Pressure:** 🔥♨️
    *   **High Pressure Zone:** Yahan **sinking air** hoti hai. 👇
    *   **Effect:** Sinking air **convection** ko suppress karti hai (hawa ko upar uthne nahi deti).
	    *   **Result 1:** Aasmaan saaf rehta hai (**Clear Skies**). ☀️
	    *   **Result 2:** Clear skies + Direct sunlight = Surface **bohot zyada garam** ho jaati hai. 🔥
	    *   **Result 3:** Jo hawa neeche sink kar rahi hai, woh **Adiabatic Heating** se garam hoti hai (closer to surface -> more pressure -> compression -> heating).
    *   **Outcome:** Surface ke paas, STHPB ke neeche, ek **garam hawa ka pocket** ban jaata hai, jo upar nahi jaa paa raha (kyunki High Pressure hai). trapped ♨️

*   **The Big Shift:** 💨⬆️
    *   Jaise jaise summer aur advance karta hai, ITCZ aur North jaata hai.
    *   Finally, **STHPB aur STJ (Westerly) bhi North ki taraf shift ho jaate hain**.
    *   **Consequence:** Jaise hi High Pressure hat'ta hai, woh neeche trapped garam hawa (**warm air bodies**) suddenly **release** ho jaati hai! 💥

*   **Intense Convection & Effects:** 🌪️
    *   Released garam hawa **bohot tezi se upar uthti hai** -> **Intense Convection**. ⬆️⬆️
    *   **Kahan Kahan?** Yeh hota hai over:
        *   Tibetan Plateau
        *   North India
        *   Iran, Pakistan, Afghanistan
        *   Saudi Arabia
        *   North Africa (Algeria, Libya etc.) 🌍
    *   **Surface Level Effect:**
        *   Intense convection = Strong **Low Pressure** areas develop.
        *   Low pressure -> **Convergent** wind system (hawa center ki taraf aati hai).
        *   Northern Hemisphere mein convergent winds **anti-clockwise** ghoomti hain (**Cyclonic Circulation**). 🌀
        *   Agar inki speed 60 km/hr se kam ho, toh inhe **Disturbances** kehte hain. (Ye Summer WDs se linked hain, aage aayega).
    *   **Upper Air Level Effect (at Tropopause):**
        *   Jo hawa tezi se upar uthi hai, woh **Tropopause** (atmosphere ki upper limit) pe pahunch kar **diverge** karegi (failegi).
        *   Surface pe Cyclonic (Low) -> Upper Air mein **Anticyclonic** (High).
        *   Northern Hemisphere mein Anticyclonic circulation **clockwise** hota hai. <0xF0><0x9F><0x95><0xB1>️
        *   Yeh Anticyclones Tibet/N.India aur China ke upar Tropopause level pe bante hain.

*   **STEJ ka Janam (Genesis):** ✨
    *   Yeh upper air divergence North ki taraf thoda blocked rehta hai.
    *   Isliye, in Anticyclones ki **southward branches** merge ho jaati hain.
    *   Result: Ek **high-velocity upper air wind system** banta hai jo **East se West** ki taraf flow karta hai (Easterly).
    *   Isi ko **Subtropical Easterly Jet Stream (STEJ)** kehte hain! 🌬️➡️

### **STEJ ka Role Monsoon ko Affect Karne Mein** 💪☔️

*   **Kab Active Hota Hai:** Jab STHPB aur STJ (Westerly) North mein shift ho jaate hain (Mid-June ke aas paas, Summer Solstice time).
*   **Kaise Madad Karta Hai:**
    *   Yaad karo, **Monsoon Cell** = Extended Southern Hadley Cell. Iske do parts hain:
        *   Surface Flow (SE/SW winds) - Isko **Somali Jet** help karta hai.
        *   Upper Air Return Flow - Isko **STEJ** help karta hai!
    *   STEJ (diagram mein blue) Monsoon Cell ke upper air circulation (diagram mein orange) ko **dhakka deta hai (pushes)**, usko aur **tez** banata hai. 💨💨

*   **Chain Reaction:** ⛓️
    1.  STEJ ne Upper Air Flow ko **strengthen** kiya 💪.
    2.  Tez Upper Air Flow -> Cell ke doosre end (Southern Hemisphere STHPB/Mascarene High) pe **stronger subsidence** (hawa ka neeche utarna) hoga 👇.
    3.  Stronger Subsidence -> **Mascarene High** aur **strong** ho jaayega 📈.
    4.  Stronger Mascarene High -> **SE Trade Winds** aur **strong** hongi.
    5.  Stronger SE Trades + Somali Jet ka push -> India mein enter hone wali **SW Monsoon winds** aur **strong** hongi! 🌧️🇮🇳
    *   **Bottom Line:** STEJ overall **Monsoon Circulation ki strength** badhata hai.

*   **Burst of the Monsoon:** 💥🌧️
    *   Jab STEJ Peninsular India ke upar theek se develop ho jaata hai, tabhi Monsoon **bohot intensity** ke saath India mein enter karta hai.
    *   Is phenomenon ko **"Burst of the Indian Monsoon"** kehte hain.
    *   **Important Note:** STEJ monsoon ki *strength* decide karta hai, *lekin* ye **heavy rainfall ki guarantee nahi hai**. Rainfall kitni hogi (quality/quantity) woh hawa mein **moisture** kitna hai uspe depend karta hai. Moisture availability **Indian Ocean Dipole (IOD)** jaise factors pe depend karti hai (jo aage ke lectures mein aayega).

### **Summer Western Disturbances (WDs)** 🏜️➡️🌧️

*   **STEJ se Connection:** Yaad hai STEJ banne se pehle intense heating se surface pe Low Pressure areas bane the? Jo anti-clockwise ghoom rahe the (Disturbances)?
*   **Naamकरण (Naming):** Jo disturbances **Iran, Pakistan, Afghanistan** (India ke West mein) bante hain, unhe **Western Disturbances (of Summers)** kehte hain. ⬅️
*   **Nature:**
    *   Surface pe **Cyclonic Circulation** (Anti-clockwise) 🌀.
    *   Unn landmasses pe bante hain jo STJ (Westerly) ke North shift hone ke baad **intensely heat** hue the 🔥.

*   **Inka Safar (Journey & Impact):**
    1.  **Formation:** Iran, Pak, Afg pe develop hote hain.
    2.  **Rotation:** Anti-clockwise ghoomte hain.
    3.  **Moisture Pickup:** Chalte hue **Persian Gulf, Gulf of Oman, Northern Arabian Sea** se moisture uthate hain 💧.
    4.  **Movement:** Dheere dheere **East ki taraf** travel karte hain ➡️. Hawa upar uthti hai, Tropopause pe right deflect hoke (Coriolis) Clockwise upper circulation banati hai.
    5.  **Cloud Formation:** Moisture add karte hain, jaise Sulaiman Range (Pak-Afg border) pe clouds banate hain ☁️.
    6.  **Timing:** Normally ye **Mid-June** (21st June aas paas) tak hota hai.
        *   *(Context Note):* Video mein mention kiya gaya hai ki pichle kuch saal (last year, current year) sab kuch **delay** hua hai (shayad Polar Vortex ke late descent ke kaaran), toh WDs bhi late aaye (e.g., last year Mid-July mein).
    7.  **Meeting Point:** Ye Summer WDs **North-West India** mein aakar Monsoon ki **Arabian Sea Branch** aur **Bay of Bengal Branch** ke saath **merge** ho jaate hain.
    8.  **Final Result:** Teen sources se moisture ikattha hone par **NW India mein heavy rainfall** hoti hai. 🌧️🌧️🌧️

### **Monsoon Trough** 📉

*   **Kya Hai:** Garmiyon mein **ITCZ** (jo ki ek Low Pressure belt hai) Northward shift ho jaata hai, Tropic of Cancer ke bhi North mein extend ho jaata hai.
*   **Naam Kyun:** Jab Pressure ko chart pe plot karte hain (Highs and Lows), toh yeh Low Pressure ka extended belt ek **gaddhe (trough)** jaisa dikhta hai. Isliye isse **Monsoon Trough** kehte hain.

### **Final Summary Points** ✨

*   **STEJ:** Monsoon cell ke **upper circulation ko strong** karta hai -> Mascarene High strong -> Surface winds (SW Monsoon) strong -> **Overall Monsoon Strength Badhti hai**. 💪
*   **Summer WDs:** STEJ formation se judi **surface low pressure systems** hain jo West se aakar NW India mein **extra moisture** laati hain aur rainfall badhati hain. 💧➡️🌧️
*   Yeh dono factors Indian Monsoon ki **strength** ko determine karne mein **bohot important** hain.
