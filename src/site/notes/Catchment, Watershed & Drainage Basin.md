# Catchment, Watershed & Drainage Basin — Concept Clarity

## ✅ TL;DR Style

| Term         | Meaning (Hinglish)                                                                 | Keywords             |
|--------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------|
| **Catchment** | Jahan ka bhi **rainwater collect hokar ek specific stream/river** me chala jata hai | "Collect hone wala"   |
| **Watershed** | **Boundary line** jo do alag-alag catchments ko divide karti hai                    | "Divide karne wali"   |
| **Drainage Basin** | Sum total of **all catchments** jo kisi **entire river system** me contribute karte hain | "System ka total area" |

---

## ✍️ Conceptual Trigger:

> **Catchment** → Jahan ka bhi rainwater **wo nadi collect** karti hai  
> **Watershed** → Boundary **do catchments ke beech ki**  
> **Basin** → Sum total of **all catchments** of a **river system**

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic:

> **Catchment me paani ikattha hota hai**,  
> **Watershed us boundary ko batata hai**,  
> **Basin poore system ko samet leta hai**.

---

## 🌊 Visual Example:

- **Catchment** = Sirf Yamuna ka area jahan se paani Yamuna me jaata hai  
- **Watershed** = Yamuna aur Chambal ke catchments ke beech ka divide  
- **Drainage Basin** = Pura Ganga system (Yamuna + Ghaghara + Kosi + Chambal etc.)

---

## 🧭 Hierarchy:

> **Drainage Basin > Catchment Area > Watershed**
