# 🌍 Sudan Type (Savanna) Climate  

## 📍 Location  
➡️ **Tropics ke beech (10°–20° latitude)**  
➡️ **Africa (Sudan, Kenya, Tanzania), Brazil, Australia, India ke kuch parts** 

- **llanos, Campos**

## 🌡️ Climate Features  
➡️ **2 Main Seasons:**  
   - ☔ **Summer (Wet)** → Moderate baarish (75-130cm)  
   - 🔥 **Winter (Dry)** → Bilkul sukhapan  
➡️ **Temperature:** Hamesha warm-hot (25°C – 35°C)  
➡️ **Rainfall:** Mostly **Convectional** (dopahar me garam hawa upar jaake baarish)  

## 🌿 Vegetation (Jungle & Ped-paudhe)  
➡️ **Savanna Grasslands** → Coarse ghaas + scattered trees  
➡️ **Xerophytic trees:** Acacia, Baobab  
➡️ **Wildfires 🔥 common hain** → Ped kam hote hain, ghaas ke seeds survive kar lete hain  

## 🦁 Wildlife  
➡️ **Big Five:** [[Lion\|Lion]], Elephant, Rhinoceros, Buffalo, Leopard  
➡️ Giraffe, Zebra, Cheetah, Hyena, Hippo, Crocodile  

## 🚜 Human Life & Agriculture  
➡️ **Nomadic tribes:** [[Maasai\|Maasai]] (Kenya, Tanzania)  
➡️ **Farming:** Maize, Millet, Groundnut  
➡️ **Cattle Ranching:** Gaay, Bhains, Bakri charana  

## 🏞️ Open Grasslands kyon zaroori hain?  
### **1️⃣ Predators (Sher, Cheetah, Hyena) 🦁🐆**  
➡️ **Hunting easy hoti hai** (Open space me shikar dikhta hai)  
➡️ **High-speed chase possible** (Jungle me speed slow ho jaati)  
➡️ **Group hunting strategies** (Sher(Lions primarily hunt in groups) aur Wild Dogs ke liye perfect)  

### **2️⃣ Herbivores (Giraffe, Zebra, Elephant) 🦓🐘**  
➡️ **Danger detect karne ke liye** (Jungle me shikari chip sakte hain)  
➡️ **Fast escape possible** (Jungle me bhaagna risky)  
➡️ **Ghaas aur food supply available** (Large herds survive kar sakti hain)  

### **3️⃣ Migration aur Large Herds 🏞️**  
➡️ **Great Migration (Africa: [[Maasai\|Maasai]] Mara & Serengeti)**  
➡️ **Large Herds → Safety in numbers**  

### **4️⃣ Waterholes aur Open Area Connection 🌊**  
➡️ **Waterholes pe sab animals aate hain** (Dry season me important)  
➡️ **Ecosystem ka balance banta hai** (Predators & prey ke beech)  

## 🔥 Productivity Comparison  
➡️ **Tropical Evergreen** → 🔝 Sabse zyada  
➡️ **Monsoon Forests** → Savanna se better  
➡️ **Savanna** → Moderate (Garmi + seasonal baarish)  
➡️ **Desert** → Sabse kam  

✅ **Savanna ek transition zone hai Tropical Jungle aur Desert ke beech!**

# 🌍 India me Savanna Climate (Aw)  

## 📍 Location  
➡️ **Peninsular India me Tropical Savanna Climate milta hai:**  
   - **Maharashtra:** Nagpur, Vidarbha region  
   - **Karnataka:** Interior regions  
   - **Andhra Pradesh & Telangana:** Hyderabad aur nearby regions  
   - **Tamil Nadu:** Chennai ke aaspaas  

## 🌡️ Climate Features  
➡️ **Distinct Wet & Dry Season**  
   - ☔ **Summer (Wet):** Convectional Rainfall  
   - 🔥 **Winter (Dry):** Sukhapan  
➡️ **Temperature:** Hamesha warm-hot (25°C – 35°C)  
➡️ **Rainfall:** 750-1300mm, mostly monsoonal  

## 🌿 Vegetation (Jungle & Ped-paudhe)  
➡️ **Tropical Dry Deciduous Forests** 🌳  
➡️ **Scattered trees + Grasslands** 🌾  
➡️ **Common trees:** Acacia, Palash, Teak  

## 🦁 Wildlife  
➡️ **Velavadar Blackbuck Sanctuary (Gujarat)** → Pure Grasslands  
➡️ **Kanha & Pench National Park (MP)** → Open Grasslands + Dry Forests  
➡️ **Bandipur (Karnataka), Simlipal (Odisha)** → Savanna-type scattered trees  

## 🔥 Why Not Like African Savanna?  
🚫 **India me zyada rainfall** → Dense vegetation hoti hai  
🚫 **Wildfires frequent nahi** → Grasslands fully open nahi rehte  
🚫 **Human activity (Farming, Grazing)** → Natural savanna disturbed  

✅ **Best Example:** **Velavadar (Gujarat) & Kanha (MP) ke grasslands, jo African Savanna se kaafi milte hain!**
