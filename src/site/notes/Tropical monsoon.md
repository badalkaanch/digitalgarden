# 🌧️ Tropical Monsoon Climate  

## 📍 Location  
➡️ **Köppen Classification:** Am (Tropical Monsoon Climate)  
➡️ **Latitude:** **5°-30° N/S**  
➡️ **Main Regions:**  
   - **South & Southeast Asia** → India, Bangladesh, Myanmar, Thailand, Vietnam 🇮🇳🇧🇩🇲🇲  
   - **West Africa** → Nigeria, Ghana 🌍  
   - **Northern Australia** 🇦🇺  

## 🌡️ Climate Features  
➡️ **High Temperature Year-Round** 🔥 → **25-35°C**  
➡️ **Seasonal Rainfall ☔:** **100-250 cm/year**  
➡️ **Distinct Wet & Dry Season**  
   - 🌧️ **Wet (Monsoon) Season:** Heavy rainfall due to monsoon winds  
   - ☀️ **Dry Season:** Cool & dry due to retreating monsoon  
➡️ **Rainfall Variability** → Some years excess, some years drought  
➡️ **Monsoon Onset & Retreat** → Controlled by ITCZ (Inter-Tropical Convergence Zone)  

## 🌿 Vegetation (Monsoon Forests)  
➡️ **Tropical Deciduous Forests** 🌳 → Shed leaves in dry season  
➡️ **Common Trees:** Sal, Teak, Bamboo, Sandalwood  
➡️ **More Open & Less Dense than Equatorial Rainforest**  
➡️ **Soil Fertility 🌱** → Rich Alluvial Soil, Good for Agriculture  

## 🦁 Wildlife  
➡️ **Mammals:** Bengal Tiger, Asian Elephant, Gaur, Sloth Bear  
➡️ **Birds:** Peacocks, Hornbills, Parakeets  
➡️ **Reptiles:** Cobras, Monitor Lizards  

## 🏡 Human Activity & Challenges  
➡️ **Intensive Agriculture 🚜** → Rice, Sugarcane, Jute, Tea  
➡️ **Floods & Droughts** → Erratic Monsoon Patterns  
➡️ **Deforestation & Urbanization 🏙️**  
➡️ **Cyclones 🌪️** → Coastal monsoon regions highly vulnerable
