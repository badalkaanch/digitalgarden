### Detailed Class Notes: Somali Jet & Indian Monsoon  Monsoon Factors Series - Part 1 🌧️

**1. Introduction & Context**

*   Sabko Warm Welcome! 🤗 Finally Monsoons aa gaye hain!
*   Iss series mein hum **Indian Monsoons** ko affect karne wale **saare factors** discuss karenge.
*   Let's start with the first one: **The Somali Jet** 💨.

**2. Basic Monsoon Mechanism: Role of Mascarene High**

*   Hum sab jaante hain ki **Subtropical High Pressure Belt (STHPB)** ka woh hissa (part) jo **Southern Indian Ocean** mein hai...
    *   Usse **Mascarene High** kehte hain.
    *   Yeh **Monsoonal Winds** ke liye responsible hai jo India mein **Summers mein rainfall** laati hain 🇮🇳☀️🌧️.
*   **Why "Mascarene High"?** 🤔
    *   Kyunki yahan teen islands hain: **Rodrigues, Mauritius, and Reunion** 🏝️.
    *   Inko collectively **Mascarene Islands** bolte hain.
*   Southern Indian Ocean mein High Pressure ka yeh patch **winds initiate** karta hai.

**3. Role of ITCZ Shift & Coriolis Force**

*   Normally, **South East (SE) Trade Winds** **ITCZ (Inter Tropical Convergence Zone)** aur **STHPB** ke beech flow karti hain.
*   **Summers mein kya hota hai?** ☀️
    *   **ITCZ Northwards extend** hota hai (India ki taraf shift hota hai).
    *   Iske kaaran, **SE Trade Winds Equator cross** karti hain 🌍.
*   **Equator Cross karne ke baad:**
    *   Winds **South Westerly** ban jaati hain.
    *   **Kyun?** Because **Coriolis Force** ka direction **Northern Hemisphere** mein change ho jaata hai -> Winds ko **Right** ki taraf deflect karta hai.
*   Yeh process Monsoon ka **Surface Wind System** banata hai, jo India mein garmiyon mein baarish laata hai.

**4. The Somali Jet Connection: Southern Africa High Pressure**

*   Ab dekho, STHPB ka jo hissa **Southern part of Africa** ke upar extend hota hai...
    *   Wahan bhi ek **strong High Pressure** develop hota hai.
    *   Yeh essentially usi High Pressure Belt ka **extension** hai.
*   **High Pressure = Divergence** 🌬️
    *   High Pressure ek **Divergence** ka center hota hai (hawa bahar nikalti hai).
    *   Southern Africa ke surface par ek **strong anti-cyclonic circulation** (Clockwise in Southern Hemisphere) rehta hai.
*   Yeh **diverging winds** Southern Hemisphere mein **Left** ki taraf deflect hoti hain (Coriolis effect).

**5. Funneling Effect & Speed Gain**

*   Inn diverging winds ka woh hissa jo **African mainland aur Madagascar** ke beech se **==funnel==** hota hai (squeeze hota hai)...
    *   Kyunki woh ek **tighter space** se pass hone par force hoti hain...
    *   Unki **speed badh jaati hai** 🚀!
    *   **Example:** Jaise hum hose pipe par anghutha (thumb) laga kar paani ka pressure badhaate hain 👍.
*   Yeh forcing air ko thoda **altitude gain** karne par bhi majboor karti hai (thoda upar uthti hai).

**6. Formation of the Somali Jet**

*   Yeh **fast flowing wind** jo funnel ho rahi hai...
    *   Speed pakad leti hai (picks up speed).
    *   Kuch height gain karti hai (lagbhag **1.5 Kilometers above the surface**).
*   **Journey & Deflection:**
    *   Jab tak yeh Southern Hemisphere mein hai, yeh constantly **Left** ki taraf deflect hoti rehti hai.
    *   **Upon crossing the Equator:** Jaise hi Equator cross karti hai...
        *   **Coriolis Force** ka direction badalta hai (Northern Hemisphere rules apply).
        *   Ab yeh **Right** ki taraf deflect hone lagti hai.
    *   Yeh **Right deflection** **Coast of Somalia** ke paas (along) hota hai 🇸🇴.
*   **Definition:** Isliye, yeh **Low-Level** (kam unchai wali), **High-Velocity** (tez speed wali) wind, jo lagbhag **1.5 km altitude** par beh rahi hai, usse **Somali Jet** kehte hain.

**7. Somali Jet's Beneficial Impact on Indian Monsoon**

*   **Somali Jet** Indian Monsoon ke liye kaafi **beneficial** (faydemand) saabit hota hai 💪.
*   **Kaise?**
    *   Yeh **Monsoonal Surface Wind System** ko **Strengthen** (mazboot) karta hai.
    *   Specifically, yeh **SE Trade Winds** ko strong karta hai (before crossing equator).
    *   Aur **South Westerly** winds ko bhi strong karta hai (after crossing equator).
*   **Important Advantage:** ✨
    *   Kyunki yeh ek **Low-Level Jet** hai (sirf ~1.5 km unchai par)...
    *   Yeh Monsoon ke **Upper Air Circulation** (jo bahut upar atmosphere mein chalta hai, like Tropical Easterly Jet) ke saath **interfere nahi karta**.
    *   Isliye, yeh **surface wind system ko strengthen** karne ke liye bahut accha hai ✅.
*   **Conclusion:** Somali Jet Indian Monsoons ko affect karne wale **very crucial factors** mein se ek hai.
