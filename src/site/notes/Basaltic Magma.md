#### 2. **Basic / Basaltic / Shield Lava**

- **Viscosity**: Low (fluid, fast flow)
- **Temp**: Hotter (~1000°C)
- **Silica**: Low → gentle
- **Color**: Dark
- **Resulting volcano**: **Shield volcano** (broad, flat dome)
- **Eruption**: Quiet, spread in thin sheets
- **Location**: **Divergent boundaries**

**Mnemonic:**  
"**Basalt = Broad shields** → fast, calm, covers vast areas"
