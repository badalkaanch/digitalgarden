## What are Rossby Waves?
- Large-scale waves in the atmosphere and oceans.
- Caused mainly by Earth's rotation (Coriolis effect).
- Help transport heat, momentum, and affect weather patterns.

## Where do Rossby Waves Form?
- **Mid-latitudes** (30°–60° N/S) 🌎
- Along **Jet Streams** ✈️
- Areas with strong **temperature gradients** (like between polar & tropical regions) ❄️☀️
- In **oceans** as well as in **atmosphere** 🌊☁️

## Why Not Everywhere?
- Near the **equator**, Coriolis force is weak ➔ Classical Rossby waves don't form easily ❌
- Special cases: **[[Equatorial Rossby Waves\|Equatorial Rossby Waves]]** (different dynamics) ➡️ can form around equator.

## Key Factors for Formation
- **Earth's rotation** (Coriolis force) 🌀
- **Temperature contrasts** (thermal gradients) ♨️
- **Background flow** (jet streams, ocean currents) ➡️
