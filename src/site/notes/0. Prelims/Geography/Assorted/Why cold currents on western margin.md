### **Logic in Simple Words:**

1. **Trade winds** blow from **east to west** → they **push warm surface water westward**, **away from the western coast** (offshore movement).
2. Is movement se western coast ke paas **vacuum** ya khaali space create hota hai.
3. Us khaali jagah ko bharne ke liye, **deep ocean ka cold water** upar uthta hai → **this is upwelling**.
4. Result: **Western margins of continents** get **cold ocean currents**.
