Apan ko pata hai ki ==apan solar day ke hisab se chalte Hain== -> aur apan ko pata hai ki ==din mein 24 ghante== hote Hain-> hence-> ==Solar Day Is 24 Hours== and ***==Sidereal Day Is Shorter==***.

Basically 
- **Sidereal Day (23h 56m 4s)** → Earth **rotates 360°** relative to **distant stars**.
- **Solar/[[Synodic\|Synodic]] Day (24h)** → Because Earth moves **~1° in orbit**(revolution), it needs an **extra ~4 minutes** to align with the **Sun** again.

So, if you use a **distant star** as a reference, you count **one full rotation in ~23h 56m**. But if you use the **Sun**, you must wait **4 more minutes**, making a full day **24h**.

## Sidereal Year

Sidereal day is shorter because Earth moves forward in orbit daily.

==**Sidereal year is *longer*(than Solar/Synodic)**== because Earth’s orbit has to complete more to adjust for precession.

Mnemonic: din Aur sal mein Chhota kya hai->

==din Chhota hota Hai==-> siderial day is ==shorter== than solar day.

==Sal Lamba hota Hai==-> Sidereal Year is ==longer== than Solar Year.
