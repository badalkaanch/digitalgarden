## Thermocline & ENSO – Quick UPSC Note

### Mnemonic:
"Thermo = Heat, Cline = Slope" → Heat slope shift in ocean depth

---

### Thermocline – Basic Concept

- Ek mid-layer in oceans/lakes jahan temperature sharply decrease karta hai with depth  
- Separates:  
  - Warm upper layer (epilimnion)  
  - Cold deep layer (hypolimnion)

---

### Normal Pacific Conditions

- Trade winds (E ➡️ W) push warm surface water to West Pacific  
- Australia/Indonesia side:
  - Warm water accumulate ➡️ Low pressure  
  - ➡️ Shallow thermocline (compressed from above)  
- Peru side:
  - Upwelling of cold water ➡️ High pressure  
  - ➡️ Deep thermocline

---

### El Niño Conditions

- Trade winds weaken/reverse  
- Warm water shift karta hai East Pacific (Peru)  
- ➤ Eastern Pacific warms up  
- ➤ Thermocline flatten ho jaati hai across Pacific  
- ➤ Upwelling block ho jaata hai ➡️ marine productivity girti hai  
- ➤ Walker circulation weakens or reverses

### Quick Revision Pointers (UPSC-style)

- Thermocline moves vertically, not horizontally  
- Arrow in ENSO diagrams = direction of thermocline tilt, not flow  
- ENSO disrupts normal thermocline slope, affecting:
  - Upwelling
  - Fishery yield
  - Rainfall patterns (e.g., Indian Monsoon)
