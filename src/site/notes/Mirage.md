# 🔮 Mirage: Ek Optical Illusion

## 📌 Definition
Mirage ek **optical illusion** hai jo tab hoti hai jab **light rays bend** karti hain due to **temperature gradient** in the atmosphere.

---

## 🔬 Mechanism

| Step | Process                                  | Explanation                                                                 |
|------|------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1️⃣   | **Refraction**                          | Light bends jab woh alag-alag density wale air layers se guzarti hai        |
| 2️⃣   | **Temperature Gradient**               | Neeche wali hot air → less dense, Upar wali cool air → more dense          |
| 3️⃣   | **Total Internal Reflection**           | Light critical angle par reflect ho jaati hai, upar ki taraf bend karti hai|
| 4️⃣   | **Illusion Formation**                  | Dimaag light ko straight assume karta hai → dikhta hai jaise pani ho       |

---

## 🌡️ Types of Mirage

| Type               | Region             | Object Appears        | Cause                                     |
|--------------------|--------------------|------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| 🌵 **Inferior**     | Deserts, Roads     | Below original         | Hot ground air bends light upward         |
| ❄️ **Superior**     | Polar/Cold Regions | Above original         | Cold air below, warm air above (inversion)|
| 🏰 **Fata Morgana** | Horizon, Seas      | Distorted/Stacked      | Multiple temperature inversions           |

---

## 🧠 Keywords  
`Refraction`, `Total Internal Reflection`, `Temperature Gradient`, `Optical Illusion`, `Density Difference`
